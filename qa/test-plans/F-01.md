## Happy Path

### F-01-TC-1: Homepage loads with correct title and structure

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:8080/`
2. Wait for page to fully load (DOMContentLoaded + no pending network requests)

**Expected:**
- HTTP status 200
- Page `<title>` contains `~/nikila.dev`
- Terminal ASCII banner (`╔══════════════════════════════════════╗`) is visible
- `$ whoami` block renders with text `nikila fernando`
- Section table renders with exactly 5 rows: `devops/`, `til/`, `tools/`, `notes/`, `projects/`
- "Recent Posts" heading is visible
- At least one post link is listed under Recent Posts
- No JavaScript console errors

**Screenshot checkpoints:** initial load, scroll to Recent Posts section, footer

---

## Negative Cases

### F-01-TC-5: Draft posts do not appear on homepage

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:8080/`
2. Check Recent Posts list

**Expected:**
- Posts with `draft: true` in frontmatter are NOT listed
- Only published posts (`draft: false` or no draft field) appear

**Note (traceability):** `Plugin.RemoveDrafts()` is enabled in quartz.config.ts — this verifies it works end-to-end.
